## What it is

Sabina Was Here sells original art and the apparel that grows out of it. The site works as a gallery and a shop at once: each painting and shirt gets its own page with a full description and price, and the layout gets out of the way so the pieces carry the page.

Zathena Ventures designed, built and hosts the site.

## Features

### Art and apparel
- **Paintings**: original acrylic on canvas pieces, led by the CATS Collection.
- **T-shirts**: limited edition apparel, including collaborations and original designs.
- **Product pages**: a dedicated page for every item with description and pricing.
- **Collector's bundles**: buy a whole collection in one go.
- **More on the way**: pages for new categories, such as Shoppers and Home Décor, are set up for future drops.

### Shopping
- **Cart that remembers**: items stay in the cart between visits, with quantity changes and removal.
- **Simple checkout flow**: a short path from cart to order.

### Look and feel
- **Responsive**: built for phones, tablets and desktops.
- **Dark and light mode**: switch themes to suit the room.
- **Help and scroll-to-top**: small helpers that make longer pages easy to move through.
